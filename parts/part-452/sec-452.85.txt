Subpart H—Right To Vote
## § 452.85 Reasonable qualifications on right to vote.

§ 452.85 Reasonable qualifications on right to vote.

The basic right of members to vote in elections of the labor organization may be qualified by reasonable rules and regulations in its constitution and bylaws. 44

44 Act, sec. 101(a)(1).
