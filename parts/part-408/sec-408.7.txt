Title 29 CFR — Part 408—LABOR ORGANIZATION TRUSTEESHIP REPORTS
## § 408.7 Terminal trusteeship financial report.

§ 408.7 Terminal trusteeship financial report.

Each labor organization which has assumed trusteeship over a subordinate labor organization shall file within 90 days after the termination of such trusteeship on behalf of the subordinate labor organization a terminal financial report, and one copy, with the Office of Labor-Management Standards, on Form LM-2 Long Form or Form LM-2 and in conformance with the requirements of part 403 of this chapter.
