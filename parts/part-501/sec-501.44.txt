Title 29 CFR — Part 501—ENFORCEMENT OF CONTRACTUAL OBLIGATIONS FOR TEMPORARY AGRICULTURAL WORKERS ADMITTED UNDER SECTION 218 OF THE IMMIGRATION AND NATIONALITY ACT
Review of Administrative Law Judge's Decision
## § 501.44 Additional information, if required.

§ 501.44 Additional information, if required.

Where the ARB has determined to review such decision and order, the ARB will notify each party of:

(a) The issue or issues raised;

(b) The form in which submissions must be made (e.g., briefs or oral argument); and

(c) The time within which such presentation must be submitted.
