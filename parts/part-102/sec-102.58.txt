Title 29 CFR — Part 102—RULES AND REGULATIONS, SERIES 8
Subpart C—Procedure Under Section 10(A) to (I) of the Act for the Prevention of Unfair Labor Practices
## § 102.58 Withdrawal of compliance specification.

§ 102.58 Withdrawal of compliance specification.

Any compliance specification and Notice of Hearing may be withdrawn before the hearing by the Regional Director upon the Director's own motion.
