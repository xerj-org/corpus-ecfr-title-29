Title 29 CFR — Part 102—RULES AND REGULATIONS, SERIES 8
Subpart F—Procedure for Referendum Under Section 9(e) of the Act
## § 102.87 Method of conducting balloting; postballoting procedure.

§ 102.87 Method of conducting balloting; postballoting procedure.

The method of conducting the balloting and the postballoting procedure will be governed by the provisions of § 102.69, insofar as applicable.
