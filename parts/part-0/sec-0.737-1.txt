Title 29 CFR — Part 0—ETHICS AND CONDUCT OF DEPARTMENT OF LABOR EMPLOYEES
Subpart B—Post Employment Conflict of Interest
## § 0.737-1 Applicability.

§ 0.737-1 Applicability.

This subpart is applicable to any former employee of the Department of Labor leaving Government service on or after July 1, 1979 and prior to January 1, 1991.
