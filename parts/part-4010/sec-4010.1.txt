Title 29 CFR — Part 4010—ANNUAL FINANCIAL AND ACTUARIAL INFORMATION REPORTING
## § 4010.1 Purpose and scope.

§ 4010.1 Purpose and scope.

This part prescribes the requirements for annual filings with PBGC under ERISA section 4010.
