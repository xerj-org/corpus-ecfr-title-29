Title 29 CFR — Part 4211—ALLOCATING UNFUNDED VESTED BENEFITS TO WITHDRAWING EMPLOYERS
Subpart D—Allocation Methods for Merged Multiemployer Plans
## § 4211.35 Direct attribution method for withdrawals after the initial plan year.

§ 4211.35 Direct attribution method for withdrawals after the initial plan year.

The allocation method under this section is the allocation method described in section 4211(c)(4) of ERISA.
