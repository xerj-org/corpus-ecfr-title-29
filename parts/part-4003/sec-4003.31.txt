Title 29 CFR — Part 4003—RULES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE REVIEW OF AGENCY DECISIONS
Subpart C—Reconsideration of Initial Determinations
## § 4003.31 Who may request reconsideration.

§ 4003.31 Who may request reconsideration.

Any person aggrieved by an initial determination of PBGC to which this subpart applies may request reconsideration of the initial determination.
