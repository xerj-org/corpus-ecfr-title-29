Title 29 CFR — Part 2700—PROCEDURAL RULES
Subpart G—Hearings
## § 2700.64 Exhibits.

§ 2700.64 Exhibits.

All exhibits received in evidence in a hearing or submitted for the record in any proceeding before the Commission shall be deemed part of the official record of the proceeding. The withdrawal of original exhibits may be permitted by the Commission or the ALJ, upon request and after notice to the other parties, if true copies are substituted, where practical, for the originals.
