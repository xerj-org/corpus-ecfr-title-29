Title 29 CFR — Part 2590—RULES AND REGULATIONS FOR GROUP HEALTH PLANS
Subpart B—Health Coverage Portability, Nondiscrimination, and Renewability
## § 2590.701-7 HMO affiliation period as an alternative to a preexisting condition exclusion.

§ 2590.701-7 HMO affiliation period as an alternative to a preexisting condition exclusion.

The rules for HMO affiliation periods have been superseded by the prohibition on preexisting condition exclusions. See § 2590.715-2704 for rules prohibiting the imposition of a preexisting condition exclusion.
