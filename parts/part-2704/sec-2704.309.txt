Title 29 CFR — Part 2704—IMPLEMENTATION OF THE EQUAL ACCESS TO JUSTICE ACT IN COMMISSION PROCEEDINGS
Subpart C—Procedures for Considering Applications
## § 2704.309 Judicial review.

§ 2704.309 Judicial review.

Judicial review of final Commission decisions on awards may be sought as provided in 5 U.S.C. 504(c)(2).
